# Model index

The portfolio combines several Advanced Financial Engineering assignments into one coherent modelling sequence. The most relevant topics are summarised below; detailed numerical outputs are collected in `RESULTS.md`.

| Model | Main topic | Key techniques |
|---|---|---|
| Mortgage amortisation | Mortgage cash flows | amortisation, fixed vs floating rates |
| Bond pricing & pull-to-par | Bond valuation | discounting, cash-flow valuation, pull-to-par |
| Realised return | Fixed income | coupon reinvestment, terminal wealth |
| Yield-curve bootstrapping | Term structure | discount factors, spot rates, implied forwards |
| Forwards & futures | Derivatives | no-arbitrage pricing, futures P&L |
| FX hedging | Foreign exchange | forward hedge construction |
| Currency swaps | Cross-currency risk | principal exchanges, cash-flow mapping |
| Interest-rate swap valuation | Rates derivatives | forward curve, discount curve, interpolation, NPV |
| Credit risk & CDS | Credit derivatives | hazard rates, survival probabilities, premium/protection legs |

The original models were built in Excel. Cleaned spreadsheet copies are retained separately from the public-facing summaries so that collaborator names and student identifiers are not exposed.
